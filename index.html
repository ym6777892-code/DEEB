Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from DEEB.

Ordering is simple. Browse our collection, click the "Order via WhatsApp" button on any product, and send us your details including product name, size, and delivery address. We will confirm your order within minutes.

We offer express shipping with delivery within 2-5 business days depending on your location. Local deliveries are often completed within 24-48 hours. You will receive a tracking number once your order ships.

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging. Contact us via WhatsApp to initiate a return. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Each product page includes a detailed size guide. Our sizes generally run true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit or down for a more fitted look. Contact us for personalized sizing advice.

Yes, we offer cash on delivery for select locations. You can also pay via bank transfer or mobile payment. All payment details will be shared when you place your order through WhatsApp.
